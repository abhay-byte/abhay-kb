## How to Use

1. Start with **rating 800** and solve all 31 problems
2. Move to the next rating only after finishing the current level
3. Each problem is formatted as `contestId:problemIndex` (e.g., `1903:A` means problem A from contest 1903)
4. Solve on [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/problemset) and submit your solutions
