About Me

My origin is from Palakkad, Kerala, but I was born and brought up in New Delhi. I did my schooling at Kerala Sr. Secondary School, Vikaspuri, New Delhi — completing my Class X in 2018 and Class XII in 2021. For my 9th grade, I spent a year in Kerala at Bhavans Vidya Mandir, Palakkad — moved there due to family circumstances. While there, I learned karate at a local dojo (WFSK Shotokan Karate).

In my 12th grade, I published my first app to the Play Store — Final Benchmark. Released in February 2021, it was built with Unity to benchmark Android phone performance.

Cleared JEE Mains and joined Guru Tegh Bahadur Institute of Technology (GTBIT), Delhi — ranked among India's top higher education institutions in EW India Higher Education Rankings (EWIHER) 2025-26 — pursuing my B.Tech in Information Technology (Evening Batch).

In my first year, I joined GDSC (Google Developer Student Clubs) in the Design department — my first society in college.

During my first year, I made and published my first game — Fantasy Racer — on the Play Store. It had plenty of bugs and rough edges, but it was complete.

At the end of my first year, I landed my first job as a Game Developer at Gamified Company (based in Bengaluru) — a remote role where I worked on WordSea, a Wordle-style word game. Stayed there for 2 months.

I'm decent at sports too. In school, I won medals in track and field — 100m, 200m, long jump, relay — and played football. I've also participated in inter-school zonals events.

I'm a pure vegetarian (not vegan though) — and a notoriously picky eater. I don't like vegetables despite being a vegetarian, which is ironic. My favourite food is noodles.

When I'm not coding, I love watching anime — 300+ shows and counting. My all-time favourite is One Piece (currently at episode 956).
