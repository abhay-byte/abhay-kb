# Privacy Policy for Sahur Sort

**Effective Date:** June 25, 2026  
**Developer / Studio:** Abhay Byte (Antigravity Games)

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Sahur Sort** ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our mobile application (**Sahur Sort**), available on the Google Play Store and other compatible platforms. 

By downloading, accessing, or playing Sahur Sort, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not access or use the application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information Collected Automatically (Local & Cloud Game State)
To provide core gameplay mechanics, we collect and store progression metrics:
* **Gameplay Metrics:** High scores, total sorted items, sorting accuracy, coin balances, win streaks, and unlocked achievements.
* **Storage Layer:** By default, this data is saved locally on your device storage (`PlayerPrefs`). When authenticated, progression is backed up to **Google Play Games Services (GPGS)** cloud save servers.

### B. Device & Technical Data
When you run the app, certain diagnostic metadata is generated automatically:
* Device model, Operating System version (e.g., Android / Linux), and unique device identifiers.
* Crash logs, performance metrics, and frame rate diagnostics.

### C. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics Data
Sahur Sort utilizes third-party SDKs to serve rewarded advertisements (e.g., "Watch Ad to Revive") and analyze app stability:
* **Ad Networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads):** May collect advertising IDs (AAID) and IP addresses to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific game operations:
1. **Core Gameplay & Persistence:** Maintaining your inventory, coin balance, equipped skins, and player level across sessions.
2. **Leaderboards & Social Competition:** Displaying your chosen player handle and high scores on Global and Friend leaderboards.
3. **Monetization & Revive Mechanics:** Verifying rewarded video ad completions to grant gameplay bonuses (+5 Coins Revive).
4. **App Optimization:** Analyzing crash reports to patch bugs and improve frame pacing.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We **never** sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data under the following circumstances:
* **Platform Providers:** With **Google LLC** (Google Play Games SDK) to authenticate your profile and manage cloud leaderboards.
* **Legal Compliance:** If required by applicable laws, regulations, or legal proceedings.

---

## 5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

* **Local Storage:** You can completely wipe all locally collected progression data at any time by clearing the application's storage data in your device's Settings menu or by uninstalling the application.
* **Cloud Storage:** You can manage or revoke Google Play Games permissions directly within your Google Account dashboard.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Sahur Sort is designed as a family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age without parental consent. If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected outside of permitted COPPA exceptions, we will immediately delete it.
