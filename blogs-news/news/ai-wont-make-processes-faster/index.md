# I Don't Think AI Will Make Your Processes Go Faster

> Originally published on Frederick Van Brabant's Blog — May 15, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=AI+Process+Speed+Myth)

## Summary

Drawing from classic process optimization literature like *The Toyota Way* and *The Goal*, Frederick Van Brabant argues that organizations mistakenly believe AI will speed up their processes without first addressing upstream bottlenecks. He contends that throwing AI at a slow process without understanding the root cause of delays is no different from throwing more people at the problem — and often makes things worse.
