# Web Devs Fear AI Job Displacement: State of Web Dev AI 2026 Survey

> Originally published on The Register — May 21, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=State+of+Web+Dev+AI+2026)

## Summary

The 2026 State of Web Dev AI survey by Devographics, polling 7,258 developers, reveals that nearly half of web developers fear AI will displace their jobs. The survey shows dramatic shifts since 2025 — 63% of developers now use AI to generate more than half their code, and 27% use AI for 90% or more. ChatGPT leads model usage (88.4%) while Claude wins paid subscriptions (69%), and concerns grow about junior developer skill loss as companies increasingly prefer AI spend over training.
