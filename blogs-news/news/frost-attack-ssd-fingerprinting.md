# FROST Attack: Websites Spy on Visitors by Analyzing SSD Activity

> Originally published on Ars Technica — May 31, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/16213e/e94560?text=FROST+SSD+Spy+Attack)

## Summary

Researchers developed a novel side-channel attack called FROST (Fingerprinting Remotely using OPFS-based SSD Timing) that enables malicious websites to silently profile user activity through JavaScript alone. By allocating large OPFS files (1GB+) and measuring SSD access latency with a convolutional neural network, attackers can fingerprint which apps and websites a user has open — all without cookies or traditional tracking. The technique was demonstrated on macOS and Linux, though it requires unusually large file allocations that may alert observant users.
