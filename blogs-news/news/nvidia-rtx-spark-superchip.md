# NVIDIA Unveils RTX Spark Superchip — AI PC Platform with Arm CPU, Blackwell GPU, and 128GB Unified Memory

> Originally published on NVIDIA — May 31, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=NVIDIA+RTX+Spark)

## Summary

NVIDIA announced the RTX Spark Superchip at Computex 2026, a groundbreaking single-chip platform that fuses a 20-core Arm CPU with a Blackwell RTX GPU (up to 6,144 CUDA cores) and up to 128 GB of unified memory, delivering up to 1 petaflop of FP4 AI performance. Designed for the slimmest Windows PCs and small desktops, RTX Spark enables local AI development, agentic computing, and high-end gaming in ultra-portable form factors with all-day battery life. The chip runs CUDA natively and targets developers, creators, and gamers alike.
