# DuckDuckGo Launches 'No-AI' Search Extensions as Traffic Booms

> Originally published on TechCrunch — June 1, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=DuckDuckGo+No+AI+Search)

## Summary

DuckDuckGo released dedicated browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox that let users set its AI-free search experience (noai.duckduckgo.com) as their default search engine, offering no AI answers, chat prompts, or AI-generated images in results. The launch comes as DuckDuckGo's traffic surges following Google's AI-first overhaul of its search engine — web visits to the no-AI page were up nearly 30% week-over-week, with U.S. iOS app installs peaking at 69.9% week-over-week growth. The trend suggests a sustained user shift away from AI-integrated search.
