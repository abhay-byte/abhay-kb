Open-source hardware pioneer Adafruit received a demand letter from Fenwick & West, the law firm representing Flux.ai, an electronic design automation startup. The letter demands that Adafruit take down certain content and IP, raising concerns in the open-source hardware community about legal pressure on OSHW (Open Source Hardware) projects.
