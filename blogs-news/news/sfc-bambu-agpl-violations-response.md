# SFC Launches Multi-Pronged Response to Bambu's AGPLv3 Violations
> Originally published on LWN.net — May 25, 2026
![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=AGPL+Bambu+License+Violation)
## Summary
The Software Freedom Conservancy (SFC) published a comprehensive response to Bambu Lab's AGPLv3 violations in its 3D printer software. Bambu has failed to provide source code for AGPLv3-licensed modifications and threatened Paweł Jarczak, creator of a legitimate fork. The SFC launched the baltobu project to reverse-engineer and reimplement the Bambu code while also hosting the Orca Slicer fork, pledging a multi-pronged effort for both short-term consumer assistance and long-term right-to-repair improvements.
