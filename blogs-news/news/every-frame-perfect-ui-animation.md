## Summary

Nikita Prokopov's widely-discussed essay (586 points on Hacker News) argues that UI quality should be judged by screenshots taken at any moment — not just start and end states. Drawing inspiration from Wayland's "every frame is perfect" goal, the piece dissects real-world animation failures in Safari, Apple Photos, YouTube, and other apps where desynchronized animations, white flashes between screens, and inconsistent states erode user trust. The core message: pay attention to everything in between.
