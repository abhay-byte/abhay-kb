# Uber Caps AI Tool Usage at $1,500/Month per Developer to Manage Costs

> Originally published on Simon Willison's Blog — June 3, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Uber+Caps+AI+Tool+Costs)

## Summary

After blowing its 2026 AI budget in just four months, Uber is limiting employees to $1,500 in monthly token spending per AI coding tool like Cursor or Claude Code. Simon Willison analyzes this — at two actively used tools per engineer, that's a $36,000 annual cap (roughly 11% of Uber's median $330K software engineer compensation). He notes this is a far more rational approach than the "token maxxing" leaderboards that encourage competing for maximum AI usage.

## Key Highlights

- **$1,500/month per tool** — spending on one tool doesn't affect another tool's budget
- **Policy already in effect** — instituted in recent months after the company burned through its 2026 AI budget by April
- **~11% of comp** — at two tools per engineer, the cap represents about 11% of the median $330K total compensation
- **Willison's own usage** — ~$1,000/month per provider (Anthropic + OpenAI), but only costs $100 due to subsidized individual plans
- Contrasts with "token maxxing" culture where competing for AI usage is encouraged
