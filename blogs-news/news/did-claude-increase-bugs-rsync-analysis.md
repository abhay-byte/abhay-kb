# Did Claude Increase Bugs in rsync? A Statistical Data Analysis
> Originally published on Alexis Purslane / Hacker News — June 5, 2026

## Summary

A data-driven analysis conducted a permutation test on every rsync release with bug data to answer a single question: are the Claude-assisted releases of rsync unusually buggy compared to the historical distribution? The methodology used severity-weighted bugs per 10 commits and was designed in consultation with a statistician to avoid noise from the low number of post-Claude samples.

The analysis used a rigorous approach — a simple distributional comparison rather than complex regression — and found that the key question is whether post-Claude releases fall outside the expected range of the historical bug distribution. The report sparked significant discussion on Hacker News with 270 points and 259 comments, reflecting the community's deep interest in the measurable impact of AI-assisted coding on software quality.
