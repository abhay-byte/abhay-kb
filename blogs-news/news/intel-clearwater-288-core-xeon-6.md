# Intel Launches 288-Core Clearwater Forest Xeon 6 on 18A Process

> Originally published on The Register — June 1, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Intel+288-Core+Xeon+6)

## Summary

Intel unveiled its Clearwater Forest Xeon 6+ processors at Computex 2026, featuring 288 Darkmont E-cores on the 18A (2nm-class) process. The chip uses 12 compute tiles stacked on base dies, offers SKUs from 144 to 288 cores at 330-450W TDP, and is positioned as ideal for agentic AI workloads that don't need wide vector registers. It packs 200 more cores per socket than Nvidia's Vera CPUs.
