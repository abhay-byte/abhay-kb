# Apple Decides Not to Roll Out Siri in EU After Regulatory Denial

> Originally published on Reuters — June 9, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Apple+Siri+EU+Blocked)

## Summary

Apple decided not to roll out its updated Siri AI assistant in the European Union after EU regulators denied its request for exemption from digital regulations. The EU Commission stated Apple failed to make its AI tool comply with EU regulations, marking a significant clash between US tech giants and European digital sovereignty framework. The decision affects millions of EU iPhone users and highlights the growing regulatory divide in AI deployment between markets.
