# Google AI Studio Now Lets Anyone Build Native Android Apps in Minutes

> Originally published on TechCrunch / The Verge — May 20, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=AI+Studio+Android+Apps)

## Summary

At Google I/O 2026, the company unveiled new capabilities in its web-based Google AI Studio that allow users to build native Android apps using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose through natural language prompts. The feature includes an embedded Android emulator for live preview, shrinking what typically takes weeks of setup and coding down to minutes. Google also announced an AI Studio mobile app and deeper Workspace integrations as part of its push to make "vibe coding" available to everyone.
