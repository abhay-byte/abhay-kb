# Cloudflare Turnstile Now Requires Fingerprintable WebGL, Blocking Privacy Browsers

> Originally published on hacktivis.me — May 31, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Cloudflare+Turnstile+WebGL)

## Summary

Cloudflare Turnstile, the CAPTCHA alternative used by millions of websites, now requires WebGL fingerprinting to verify users — effectively blocking WebKitGTK-based browsers and potentially other privacy-focused browsers. The change sparked major discussion on Hacker News (672 points, 366 comments), with critics arguing it defeats the purpose of a privacy-preserving CAPTCHA alternative. Even Apple's WebKit blocks this kind of tracking fingerprinting, meaning Chrome and Firefox are the only mainstream browsers unaffected.
