# Apple Courts Developers with Privacy-First AI Vision at WWDC 2026

> Originally published on The Register — June 13, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Apple+WWDC+Privacy+AI)

## Summary

At WWDC 2026, Apple presented a notably restrained AI vision centered on privacy, context-awareness, and on-device intelligence. Key announcements include a rebuilt Siri AI, the Foundation Models framework for developers, Safari's "Describe an Extension" low-code creation service, and platform improvements like 30% faster app launches and 70% faster Photos loading. The Register contrasts Apple's sober approach with the more aggressive AI positioning of Anthropic and OpenAI, noting Apple's Private Cloud Compute has even influenced Google's approach to AI privacy.
