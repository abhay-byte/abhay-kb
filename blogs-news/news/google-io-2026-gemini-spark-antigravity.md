# Google I/O 2026 Kicks Off: Gemini Spark, Antigravity 2.0, and AI-First Everything

> Originally published on AP News / TechCrunch / Engadget — May 19, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Google+I%2FO+2026)

## Summary

Google's annual developer conference I/O 2026 kicked off today with a barrage of AI announcements. CEO Sundar Pichai declared the company is "firmly in our agentic Gemini era." Key launches include Gemini Spark, a 24/7 agentic personal assistant with Gmail integration built from Gemini's base models and Google's Antigravity agentic harness; and Antigravity 2.0, an updated agentic coding application featuring a desktop app, CLI tool, and SDK for custom workflows. Android is also getting a major AI overhaul with deeper Gemini integration across the OS.
