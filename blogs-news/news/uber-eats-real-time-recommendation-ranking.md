# Uber Improves Restaurant Recommendations with Real-Time Signals and Listwise Ranking
> Originally published on InfoQ — May 22, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Uber+Eats+AI+Ranking)

## Summary

Uber updated its Uber Eats recommendation system with real-time user signals and a listwise Generative Recommender (GenRec) model. The system evolved from batch-oriented feature pipelines to transformer-based sequence modeling, reducing feature freshness from 24 hours to seconds and shifting from pointwise scoring to listwise ranking for improved contextual personalization within active browsing sessions.
