## Summary

A major supply chain attack compromised multiple npm packages under the `@redhat-cloud-services/` scope, affecting dozens of packages including chrome, frontend-components, compliance-client, and integrations-client across multiple compromised versions. The attack was detected by StepSecurity and has drawn massive attention on Hacker News with 712 points. The compromised packages could have impacted Red Hat's cloud infrastructure customers, highlighting the ongoing risks in the JavaScript npm ecosystem.
