# VoidZero Joins Cloudflare — Vite Stays Open Source, Vendor-Agnostic

> Originally published on Cloudflare Blog — June 04, 2026

![Cover](https://cf-assets.www.cloudflare.com/zkvhlag99gkb/77PFBBDzFzIVtsPi4GXm5g/ea71873167012a47518dc593033032c6/BLOG-VOID_OG.png)

## Summary

VoidZero, the company behind Vite, Vitest, Rolldown, Oxc, and Vite+, is joining Cloudflare. All team members including Evan You are moving to Cloudflare, but crucially Vite and all related projects remain MIT-licensed, open source, and vendor-agnostic. Cloudflare is committing engineering resources and $1 million to an independent Vite ecosystem fund, ensuring the foundational JS toolchain stays neutral while getting more resources to grow.
