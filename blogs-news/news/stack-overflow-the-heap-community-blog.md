# Stack Overflow Launches 'The Heap' — A Community Blog for Software Engineers

> Originally published on Stack Overflow Blog — May 11, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=The+Heap+Stack+Overflow)

## Summary

Stack Overflow launched "The Heap," a community blog open to all software engineers and technologists. Named as a programming reference to complement Stack Overflow's own stack-based name, The Heap invites community-contributed articles covering hot takes, best practices, and advice. Articles are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, and the best may be promoted to the main blog feed or newsletter. Currently a manual MVP with plans for direct submissions and voting in the future.
