# OpenAI Outlines WebRTC Architecture for Low-Latency Voice AI at Scale
> Originally published on InfoQ — May 22, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=OpenAI+Voice+AI+WebRTC)

## Summary

OpenAI detailed its relay-transceiver WebRTC architecture designed for low-latency voice AI at global scale. The new system replaces a conventional media termination model with a design better suited to Kubernetes and cloud load balancers, keeping WebRTC session state in a dedicated transceiver layer while using lightweight relays to reduce public UDP exposure and keep media routing close to users.
