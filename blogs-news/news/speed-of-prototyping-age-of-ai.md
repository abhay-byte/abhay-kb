# The Speed of Prototyping in the Age of AI
> Originally published on darylcecile.net — May 31, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Speed+of+Prototyping+in+the+Age+of+AI)

## Summary

Daryl Cecile reflects on how AI coding agents have transformed his engineering workflow, measuring an average 4x speed increase on day-to-day tasks while noting the more profound shift: the kind of work he can take on has fundamentally changed. He describes building multiple ambitious projects (a systems language called Sakoa, a notation language Kato, and a CLI tool Seal) that would have remained "nice idea, no time" before agents. The post also explores the hidden costs — needing to deliberately maintain technical dexterity by coding manually, and how planning at a more abstract level has improved his delegation skills with both agents and human engineers.
