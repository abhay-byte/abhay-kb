## Summary

Dav2d is a new fast AV1 video decoder project that garnered 395 points on Hacker News. The project draws inspiration from dav1d (the existing popular AV1 decoder) while pursuing further performance optimizations for modern hardware. The decoder aims to reduce CPU usage for AV1 playback across a wide range of devices, building on the foundation laid by the VideoLAN and FFmpeg ecosystem.
