## Summary

OpenCV 5 is one of the most important releases in the 25+ year history of the computer vision library. It introduces a new graph-based DNN engine with over 80% ONNX operator coverage, hardware acceleration for ARM, Snapdragon, and XPU platforms, LLM/VLM support, faster Python-first core integration, expanded 3D vision capabilities, and new data types. With over 86,000 GitHub stars and a million+ installs per day, OpenCV 5 modernizes the library architecture while adding support for modern AI workflows including vision-language models and transformers.
