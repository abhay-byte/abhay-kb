# AI Outperforms Law Professors in Stanford Law Study

> Originally published on Stanford Law School — June 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=AI+Law+Professors+Stanford)

## Summary

A groundbreaking Stanford Law study found that law professors overwhelmingly prefer AI-generated answers to student questions over responses written by their fellow instructors. In a blind evaluation of nearly 3,000 anonymized comparisons across 16 U.S. law schools, AI responses won 75% of head-to-head matchups. Crucially, professors flagged AI responses as pedagogically harmful only 3.5% of the time versus 12% for peer-written answers — and the AI performed comparably to the best human instructor. The study is notable because it tested legal reasoning requiring judgment and ambiguity, not just factual recall.
