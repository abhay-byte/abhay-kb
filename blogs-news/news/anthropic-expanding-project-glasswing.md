# Anthropic Expands Project Glasswing: AI Security Partnership Grows to 200+ Organizations

> Originally published on Anthropic — June 2, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Project+Glasswing)

## Summary

Anthropic expanded Project Glasswing, its AI-powered software security initiative, to approximately 150 new organizations across 15+ countries, bringing the total to over 200 partners. The new cohort includes critical infrastructure sectors like power, water, healthcare, and communications that weren't well represented initially. Partners have already found more than 10,000 high- or critical-severity vulnerabilities using Claude Mythos Preview, and Anthropic warns that within 6-12 months other AI companies will have similar capabilities — potentially without safeguards against misuse.
