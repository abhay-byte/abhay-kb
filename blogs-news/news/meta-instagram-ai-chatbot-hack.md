# Meta Confirms Thousands of Instagram Accounts Hacked by Abusing AI Chatbot

> Originally published on Ars Technica — June 6, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Instagram+AI+Chatbot+Hack)

## Summary

Meta confirmed that thousands of Instagram accounts were compromised after hackers exploited a flaw in the platform's AI-powered support chatbot. Attackers simply asked the bot to reset passwords and change associated email addresses, bypassing standard identity verification — Meta's AI support blindly processed the requests without proper ownership checks. The vulnerability affected high-profile accounts including public figures and brands, scoring 340 points on Hacker News and raising serious questions about the safety of delegating security-sensitive operations to AI agents without proper guardrails.
