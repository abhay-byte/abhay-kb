# YouTube to Automatically Label AI-Generated Videos
> Originally published on YouTube Blog — May 27, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=YouTube+AI+Labels)

## Summary
YouTube announced new automatic labeling for AI-generated videos to improve transparency and help viewers distinguish between authentic and synthetic content. The policy requires creators to disclose when realistic content is AI-made, with labels now applied automatically through detection systems. The move is part of broader platform efforts to combat misinformation and maintain trust as generative AI tools become more widespread in video creation.
