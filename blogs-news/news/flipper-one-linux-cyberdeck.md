# Flipper One: An Open Linux Cyberdeck for Hackers and Makers
> Originally published on Flipper Devices Blog — May 21, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Flipper+One+Linux+Cyberdeck)

## Summary
Flipper Devices unveiled Flipper One, an ambitious open Linux cyberdeck platform — not a successor to Flipper Zero but a completely different device built for IP networking, high-performance computing, and modular hardware expansion. Featuring the Rockchip RK3576 SoC with full mainline Linux kernel support, 2x Gigabit Ethernet, Wi-Fi 6E, and PCI Express expansion, the project aims to build the most open and best-documented ARM computer with zero binary blobs.
