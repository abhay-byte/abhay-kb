# Microsoft's Scout AI Assistant: Internal Strategy Aims to 'Make People Addicted'
> Originally published on 404 Media / disassociated.com — June 5, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=Microsoft+Scout+AI+Assistant)

## Summary

An internal Microsoft strategy document obtained by 404 Media reveals that the company's plan for its newly announced "Scout" AI personal assistant is to "make people addicted" to the tool before rolling out additional functionality. The document outlines a three-phase strategy progressing from an "addictive app" to an "agentic platform," raising concerns about Microsoft's approach to user engagement with AI products.

The revelation has sparked debate about tech industry practices around AI product design and user dependency. Microsoft's strategy mirrors historical patterns of building platform dependency — from Windows OS lock-in to cloud service integration — now being applied to AI personal assistants.
