## Summary

Alibaba successfully ported Android 16 to the RISC-V architecture, marking a significant milestone for open-source instruction set architecture adoption. The achievement strengthens Beijing's ambitions for a sovereign technology stack independent of ARM and x86. While the port demonstrates technical capability, broad adoption depends on building a RISC-V software ecosystem with compatible apps and drivers.
