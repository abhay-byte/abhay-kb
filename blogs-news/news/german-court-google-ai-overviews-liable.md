# Landmark German Court Ruling: Google Liable for False AI Overview Content

> Originally published on The Decoder — June 9, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=German+Court+AI+Liability)

## Summary

The Regional Court of Munich issued a landmark temporary injunction against Google, ruling that AI Overviews are Google's "own statements" and the company is directly liable for false or defamatory content they contain. The court found that AI Overviews create "independent, new, and substantive statements" by evaluating and combining third-party content, and existing legal protections for search engines don't apply. Google's defense that "users can check sources themselves" was rejected — the court noted studies showing almost no users click AI Overview source links, and the AI often makes claims not found in any linked sources.
