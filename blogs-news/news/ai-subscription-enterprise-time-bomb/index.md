## Summary

An analysis argues that AI subscriptions are becoming unsustainable for enterprises as providers shift to usage-based pricing. GitHub is moving Copilot to token-based AI Credits on June 1, 2026, replacing flat-rate premium requests. Microsoft has raised Microsoft 365 prices twice in four years tied to AI infrastructure costs, and OpenAI introduced a $100 Pro tier. With agentic workloads exhausting rate limits faster than ever, the article warns of vendor lock-in and rising costs that could catch enterprises off-guard.
