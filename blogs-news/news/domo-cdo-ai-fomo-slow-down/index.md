# Enough with the AI FOMO, Go Slow-Mo, Says Domo CDO
> Originally published on Hacker News — May 17, 2026

![Cover](https://placehold.co/800x400/1a1a2e/00d4ff?text=AI+FOMO+Go+Slow%2DMo)

## Summary

Domo chief design officer Chris Willis questions why more people aren't annoyed with AI companies that pushed technology upon users prematurely, causing a "tremendous amount of anxiety." He argues that AI models are "a product without a spec" — the feature spec being "it'll do anything for anyone, anyhow, in any language." Willis calls out "tokenmaxxing" as theater that doesn't change the bottom line, warning that "fear is not a durable strategy for innovating." The post scored 81 points on Hacker News.
